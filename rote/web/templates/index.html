AI agents can reason about financial exceptions.
The dangerous part is giving that reasoning direct authority to move money.

Rote is an authority layer between AI reasoning and financial actions. It decides when an AI-generated procedure has earned deterministic authority — and refuses to automate when the evidence is ambiguous.

The research result this design is built on

500 exceptions, same seed v1 — automate whenever a plan exists v2 — refuse when evidence is ambiguous
automated500184
automation coverage100%36.8%
accuracy88.0%100%
confidently wrong 60 0

The drop in automation is deliberate, not a regression. v1 automated every case and was wrong sixty times — and every safety layer passed those sixty: the gate permitted, the guard passed, the precondition held, execution was deterministic and the replay was byte-identical. The error was in meaning, and none of those layers reasons about meaning.

Five pre-registered experiments then failed to find any way to separate the ambiguous cases from evidence available before acting — fee arithmetic, its stability as data grew, merchant notes, settlement status, and the shortfall fraction. So we stopped looking for a tie-breaker and changed the policy instead.

We gave up 63% of automation coverage to remove every confident wrong action. That trade is the product.

What this is not

  • research grade: False. The world, the agent and the classifier are stand-ins written for this project. No number here is evidence about real reconciliation or about language models.
  • Rote is level with the agent on accuracy, never better — 500/500 each. It trades coverage for the removal of confident errors.
  • Not production. No payment rail, no bank, no external API, no credentials. The only mutation boundary is an in-memory simulated world.
  • No authentication. human:ops-lead-42 is a naming convention for the approver, nothing more.
