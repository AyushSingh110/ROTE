v1 automated everything and was wrong 60 times. Every safety layer passed those cases: the gate permitted, the guard passed, the precondition held, execution was deterministic and the replay was byte-identical. The error was in meaning, and none of those layers reasons about meaning.

Five experiments then failed to find any way to separate the two categories from the evidence available before acting — fee arithmetic, its stability across sample sizes, merchant notes, settlement status, and the shortfall fraction. So we stopped looking and changed the policy instead.

We gave up 63% of automation coverage to remove every confident wrong action. That trade is the product.
